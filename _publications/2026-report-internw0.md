InternW0 uses a high-capacity video expert for longer-horizon context and a lightweight action expert for faster control. It is trained on heterogeneous robot and egocentric data and evaluated in simulation and real-world scientific workflows.

I contributed as a coauthor. This entry is a collaborative technical report.
